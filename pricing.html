Billing Questions

The money bits, explained

Do I pay for the trial class?

No. The first session is free for every plan, and you only start paying if your child wants to come back.

Is there a discount for siblings?

Yes — 20% off the second child and every child after that, on any plan. Mention it when you book and we apply it automatically.

What happens during school holidays?

Regular sessions pause and billing pauses with them. Holiday camps run separately and are optional.

Can we switch plans later?

Any time, in either direction. Changes take effect from your next billing date and we never charge a fee for switching.

What if we miss a class?

Tell us before the session and we will move it. Missed classes can be made up on a Saturday within the same month.
