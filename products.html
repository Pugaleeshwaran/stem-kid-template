Kit Questions

Things parents always ask

Do I need to buy a kit to join a class?

No. Every lab session already has all the hardware on the bench. Kits are for children who want to keep building at home between classes.

Is soldering involved? Is it safe?

Nothing in the 6–9 and 9–12 kits needs soldering — everything snaps or plugs together. Soldering only appears in the teen kits, and only under mentor supervision in the lab.

What if my child gets stuck at home?

Each kit comes with a mentor helpline. Send a photo of the wiring on WhatsApp and you will get a reply the same day, usually within a couple of hours.

Can we upgrade a kit later?

Yes. The Starter Bot chassis accepts Junior Arduino parts, so nothing gets thrown away when your child moves up an age group.

How do we actually order one?

Use the enquiry button on any kit. Tell us your child's age and we will confirm stock, price and delivery before you pay anything.

✦ ✦

Not sure which kit fits your kid?

Tell us their age and what they are into. We will point you at one kit, not a catalogue.
