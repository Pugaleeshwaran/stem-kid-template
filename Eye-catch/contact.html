Before You Ask

Quick answers for busy parents

Is the trial class really free?

Yes. One full sixty-minute session, no card details, no obligation. Your child uses lab hardware and takes home whatever they coded.

My child has never coded before. Is that a problem?

That is the normal starting point. Most children who walk in have never touched a circuit or written a line of code, and the first session is designed for exactly that.

Can I stay and watch?

Please do. There is a parents' bench at the back of every lab, and most parents stay for the first session or two.

Do you do birthday parties or school groups?

Yes — build-a-robot parties for up to sixteen children, and weekday workshops for schools. Pick "Birthday party or group booking" in the form above and we will send options.

What if we need to reschedule?

Message us any time before the session and we will move it. Missed classes can be made up on a Saturday.
