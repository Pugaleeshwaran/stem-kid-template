We turn “why?” into “I built it.”

Curio Maker began in a garage with three curious kids, one blown fuse and a robot that only drove in circles. Six years later it still smells faintly of solder.

Our Story

It started with a robot that kept falling over

In 2019 our founder was helping her nephew finish a school science project. The robot was terrible. It fell over, the wires kept popping out, and it took four evenings to fix. But he did not want to stop — he wanted to know why it fell over.

That question became the whole method. We do not hand children a finished kit and a script to follow. We hand them a problem, a box of parts and a mentor who refuses to give the answer away. The falling-over part is the lesson.

Today we run weekly labs for more than 1,200 young makers, from six-year-olds snapping their first circuit together to teenagers shipping AI projects they present like founders.

What We Believe

Four rules we never break

🙌

Hands before slides

No child sits through a lecture. Parts come out of the box in the first ten minutes, every single session.

💥

Failing is the syllabus

A robot that does not work is not a bad class — it is the start of the interesting part. We debug together.

👥

Eight kids, one mentor

Small batches, always. Everyone gets their own board, their own bug and their own breakthrough.

🎤

Show your work

Every month ends with kids demoing what they made. Explaining it out loud is half the learning.

The Journey

From one garage to three labs

  1. 2019

    The garage days

    Three kids, one folding table and a soldering iron borrowed from a neighbour. First "class" ran on a Saturday morning.

  2. 2021

    First real lab

    We outgrew the garage. Twenty benches, proper Arduino kits and the first cohort of Junior Engineers.

  3. 2023

    3D printers arrive

    Maker Studio opens. Kids stop taping cardboard to their robots and start printing custom chassis parts instead.

  4. 2026

    1,200 makers and counting

    Three labs, eighteen mentors, an AI Explorers track and a showcase day that parents now book time off for.
